Training Rules & Overload

⚡ Progression Principles

Overload Rule: Increase weight only when ALL target sets are completed at the very top of the rep range with pristine technique. (Look for the "Increase next time" badge).
Unilateral Legs: On Bulgarian split squats and lunges, first build up reps, then add dumbbell weight.
Explosive Power: On jumps and swings, prioritize bar/body velocity, height, and quiet, soft landing mechanics before loading.
Reps in Reserve: Maintain 1–2 reps in reserve on all compound lifts. Training to absolute muscular failure is counterproductive for Muay Thai sharpness.

🥊 Weekly Scheduling Rules

Muay Thai Priority: Monday and Wednesday (20:00–21:30) club sessions are fixed and never moved.
Strength Spacing: Keep at least one easy or light day between Strength B and Strength C.
Order of Work: Heavy strength always precedes conditioning when performed on the same day.
Missed Sessions: If 1 strength day is missed, shift it to the next free day. If 2 are missed, toggle to the 2-Day Fallback plan for the week.
Time Crunch: Keep the first 3–4 key compound lifts; skip accessories and conditioning finishers first.
Aerobic Base: No "short version". If tight on time, move the session or skip it that week.
